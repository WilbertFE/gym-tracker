# Sera's pictures

The files in `sprites/` are **AI generated placeholders**, made on 29 Oct 2026 with Canva's image generator
(one base image, the others generated from it as a reference so the character stays the same), cut out from a flat green
background and compressed to WebP. They are low resolution (upscaled from small previews) until the full-size originals
are processed.

Whether you may use AI generated images in a published product depends on the generator's terms and on where you live.
Check Canva's terms (or the terms of whichever tool makes the final art) before you publish, and replace these files
with your own art whenever you like: put a transparent WebP named after the sprite id here, and add the id to
`AVAILABLE` in `src/lib/mascot/sprites.ts`.

Sera is an original character. The art does not use any franchise character, name or emblem.
